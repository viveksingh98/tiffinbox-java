   DDL lines end with one). In `batch` the log (1,893 lines on H2) is counted per mapping by `bsum`, never shown: the SQL logger's

   the INFO `HHH100501` line, and the harness's own `statistics:`, `jdbc:` and row lines. In `time` the per-run times, both medians and the
   ratio are **masked** (`[9 values, masked]`, `<masked>`) — they move with the JIT; the hashed line is the threshold's

   of record (the last of its three runs) are written to `.r-time.medians`, beside the captures, not hashed (the chip's numbers).
3. **A TiffinBox log** (`tview`): the lines of `TiffinBoxServer`, `org.hibernate.orm.core`, `org.hibernate.orm.connections.pooling`

5. **Hygiene, checked last over every capture**: no absolute path, no unit number, no Boot process line, no time or Boot's start-up count, no
